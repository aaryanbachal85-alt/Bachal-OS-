LAX WEBOS

Los Angeles International Airport - Operations Center

Weclome to the LAX Air Traffic & Terminal Operations WebOS. This real-time interface allows users to monitor flight radar, manage gate allocations, and parse system telemetry in this simulation.

System Controls

  • Double-click desktop icons to open Radar, Terminal, or FIDS
  • Drag the top handle of any window to re-position your airspace monitors.
  • Open the Terminal and type lax --help or status for live airport feeds.
  • Refer to the Handbook to understand how to manage operations at Los Angeles International Airport. Good Luck
